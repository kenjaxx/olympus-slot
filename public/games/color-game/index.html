    How odds work

    Three dice roll every round. A color you bet on pays 1:1 if it matches 1 die, 1:2 for 2 dice, and 1:3 if all three land on it — your stake is returned on top of the win. No match loses the stake. All 3 dice are decided on the server before they even start tumbling.
