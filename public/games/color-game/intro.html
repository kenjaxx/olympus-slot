Color Game

Pick your colors, roll the box, win on a match

How to Play

  • 1 Place a bet on any of the 6 colors — you can bet on more than one color in the same round.
  • 2 Hit Roll and watch the box tumble in 3D. It always settles on exactly one color.
  • 3 If the box lands on a color you bet on, that bet pays out 5×. Bets on the other colors are lost.
  • 4 The roll is decided entirely on the server — the browser only finds out the result after you roll.

Play-money entertainment only. No real currency, purchases, or withdrawals are involved.
