Olympus Slot

A 5×4 tumbling-reel slot with scatter free spins and multiplier orbs.

Slots Free Spins New

How to Play

  • 1 Set your bet using the slider, number field, or the ½ / 2× / Max quick buttons, then hit Spin.
  • 2 Matching clusters pay out and tumble — winning tiles clear and new ones drop in, chaining further wins in the same spin.
  • 3 A wild orb can appear on a winning tile and adds its value to that spin's multiplier.
  • 4 Land 4 or more scatter coins anywhere on the grid to trigger 10 free spins. 3 scatters is a near miss.
  • 5 Use Auto Spin to queue up multiple spins in a row — it stops automatically if your balance runs low.

Play-money entertainment only. No real currency, purchases, or withdrawals are involved.
